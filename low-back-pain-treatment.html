Low Back Pain and Its Modern Treatment: Is Recovery Possible Through Minimally Invasive Surgery?

Low back pain has become an extremely common issue in today's world. It can affect people of any age, whether they are professionals working long hours at a desk or homemakers managing daily chores. In most cases, low back pain is resolved through light exercise, physiotherapy, or medications; however, in some cases, it can indicate a more serious spine condition.

Primary Causes of Low Back Pain

  • Herniated / Slipped Disc: When the fibrous discs located between the vertebrae rupture or bulge out due to pressure, pressing against nearby nerves and causing severe pain or numbness.
  • Spinal Stenosis: Narrowing of the spinal canal in older adults, causing walking difficulties.
  • Spondylolisthesis: Slippage of one vertebra over another.
  • Poor Lifestyle: Incorrect posture, heavy lifting, and lack of exercise.

Red Flag Symptoms

If you experience any of the following symptoms along with lower back pain, you should immediately consult an expert Neurospine Surgeon:

  • Pain radiating down the legs or feet (Sciatica).
  • Weakness or numbness in the legs.
  • Loss of control over bladder or bowel functions (a potential medical emergency).

Modern Treatment: Minimally Invasive Spine Surgery (MISS)

Advanced medical technology has revolutionized spine surgery, offering smaller incisions, less blood loss, shorter hospital stays, and much faster recovery compared to traditional open surgeries.
